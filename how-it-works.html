How It Works

A straightforward process from first conversation to event day.

Consultation

We talk through your event — size, venue, vendors, and what you need help with. This is completely free and has no obligation.

Proposal & Booking

You receive a written quote . A signed agreement and deposit secure your date.

Planning Period

In the weeks (or months) before your event, we confirm details with your vendors and venue and build your day-of timeline.

Rehearsal / Final Check-In

We walk through the timeline together before the event and make any necessary last-minute adjustments.

Event Day

We are on-site managing vendors, timeline, and anything unexpected — so you and your guests can just be present.
