Dan Eror - Founder

I come from a family that absolutely loves to host. Having people over to celebrate big milestones (or just because) was always an exciting opportunity to organize an event with food, drinks, music, decorations, and more. And while it was fun, I often found myself so distracted running the show that I didn't give my family and friends enough attention.

I started an event coordinating business to give my clients the same celebration opportunities, but without the stress and constant distraction that comes with running an event.

Our services begin after most of the planning is done, so you still get the fun part of choosing the food, venue, and other aspects of the event, but allow me to take it accross the finish line while you spend time doing what's most important... enjoying the event you invested in with the people you care about most.
